About Alisha

A list built from the back seat

Alisha has driven for Uber in Austin for years. A few years in, she started asking every rider the same question during the ride: what's your favorite spot in this city?

She kept a running list of the answers. This site is that list — places recommended by the people who actually live here, not a tourism board.

She still drives, and still asks. New entries get added as riders bring them up.
